PCO WORKFLOW

What should an establishment check?

01

Hazardous-waste records

Use the currently applicable rules for generator registration, waste classification, storage, manifests, licensed transporters and TSD documentation.

02

Chemical inventory

Validate chemical names/CAS numbers against updated PICCS. Check PCL, CCO and PMPIN requirements separately.

03

New chemical controls

If benzene or vinyl chloride is handled, inspect the relevant CCO, registration obligations, exemptions and transition provisions.

04

Drafts and bills

Monitor public consultations and legislation, but never substitute draft provisions for legally effective requirements.
