ESTABLISHMENT CLASSIFICATION · DAO 2014-02 §5

Could your establishment be Category B?

Answer the operational questions below. One applicable Category B criterion can be enough. This is a preliminary screening, not an EMB determination.

Category A — no confirmed Category B criterion, subject to EMB verificationCategory B — meets one or more Table 1 criteria
01 Facility and hazardous chemicals
02 Air emission sources

Boiler ≥ 50 HP, generator ≥ 300 kW, or other sources emitting ≥ 10 tonnes/year are air-classification flags. Confirm their actual classification and stack-testing obligations with EMB.

03 Wastewater and BOD

A discharge of 30 m³/day or more, influent BOD of 3,000 mg/L or more, Annex 3 pollutants, or wastewater/septage treatment services can indicate Category B.

04 Solid waste and sludge
