03 / WHAT TO EXPECT

Online application sequence

1Register

Confirm your establishment CRS record where required; create/activate your PCO Online login.

2Prepare and upload

Choose New, Renewal or Cluster; fill your PCO profile; upload training, designation, affidavits and proofs.

3Evaluation

Monitor the dashboard and answer deficiency comments or clarification requests.

4Pay when assessed

Download the EMB Order of Payment, pay using authorized options and upload the official receipt.

5Approval and certificate

Wait for regional approval; generate/download the official certificate and record its expiry date.
